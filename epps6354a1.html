  2. Three applications in domain projects:

    NBA Betting Asssistant:

    • Purpose: Track recent performances of players against each team, highlight different factors that affected match ups such as injuries or suspensions. Allows betters to make better decisions when placing bets.

    • Functions:

      • Show recent player stats against speciifc teams (previous 10 games) as well as highlight which players were injured or missing in previous matchups.

      • Show which players are injured or suspended in current matchup.

      • Show spread, over/under, and historical data of how team has performed.

    • Design:

      • Home page that has upcoming games as well as betting props.

      • Search bar to lookup specific player and shows recent performance against upcoming team (ex: Kyrie Irving’s stats in the past 10 games against the LA Lakers).

      • Section that shows historical performance of team against the spread.

    Cooking Aid:

    • Purpose: Help in the kitchen by allowing users to feed ingredients into database and it will show popular recipes that can be made. It will also provide replacements for ingredients and a section for reviews and recommendations.

    • Functions:

      • Ingredient input: Users put in any ingredient they have and it will respond with recipes that you can create or recipes that require one more ingredient.

      • Ingredient Substitution: Provides substitutions for ingredients for people who are allergic or trying to be healthier (ex: yogurt for sour cream).

      • Forum for reviews, pictures, recommendations

      • Nutritional Information: Calories, Protein, and other info

    • Design:

      • Home page with popular recipes

      • Search bar to input ingredients and results will show recipes as well as close matches

      • Pop-up for substitutions of ingredients provided

      • Comment section that has option to leave comments, recommendations, and pcitures

    Grocery Tracker:

    • Purpose: Assist with tracking groceries and decreasing waste.

    • Functions:

      • Grocery input: Identify each grocery as well as expiry date

      • Will automatically prioritize groceries depending on which one expires the quickest

      • Creates a grocery list with items that are either finished, expired, or close to expired

    • Design:

      • Home page showing a list of groceries in pantry, fridge, and freezer

      • Foods that are close to expiration are at the top of the list

      • Separate link for grocery shopping that will show a grocery list with indications for products that are either finished, expired, or close to expiry.
